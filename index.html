Features

Everything you need to
dominate your job search

From application tracking to AI-powered resume analysis — JobFlow has every tool you need.

AI Resume Analyzer
Get an instant ATS score, skill gap analysis, and personalized suggestions powered by GPT-4 to optimize your resume for any job.
Application Tracker
Track every application with status updates, notes, interview dates, and job links. Never lose track of an opportunity again.
Analytics Dashboard
Visualize your job search performance with beautiful charts. See interview rates, offer conversions, and monthly activity trends.
Pipeline Management
Visualize your applications across a Kanban-style pipeline — from Applied to Offer. See exactly where every opportunity stands.
Smart AI Insights
Get personalized recommendations on which jobs to apply for, how to improve your profile, and what skills to develop next.
Secure & Private
Your data is encrypted and never shared. Full JWT authentication with bcrypt password hashing keeps your information safe.
